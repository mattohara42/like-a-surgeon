# M3 gate: notes for the first real reader

The gate in `BUILD_PLAN.md` is that Matt's 13-year-old uses the map without
instruction and gets somewhere. This page is for Matt, to read before that
session, so the session tests the map rather than the explanation.

## Set-up

Use the live site on a laptop at about 1280x800, the size the map is
designed for. Before he sits down, clear the site's stored settings (or use
a private window), so he sees the first-visit state: legend open, lineage
lanes, Teen register. Don't open anything for him.

## What to hold back

Don't explain the dots, the lines, the lanes, the legend, the search box, or
"Arrange by". Don't say "click on something". If he asks what something is,
the useful answer is "what do you think it is?", and the useful record is
his answer. Each question he has to ask is a finding about the map.

## What to watch for

Write down the first thing he clicks and how long it took him to click
anything at all. The design bets that a named dot invites a click within
seconds (SPEC.md, "Success test").

Notice whether he finds the edges, not just the dots. The lines are the
reading surface of the whole project. If he only ever opens artists, the
connection rows at the bottom of each artist panel are not doing their job.

Notice whether he reads "What to listen for" and then does something with
it: opens the YouTube link, hums, says "oh". That paragraph is the most
valuable text in the dataset, and this is the only real test it gets.

Notice whether he follows a line sideways into a lineage he wasn't starting
from. SPEC.md's success test is a genre he thought he didn't like.

Notice whether the confidence tiers mean anything to him. A good sign is
any remark about a dotted line or "our reading". A bad sign is none at all
after twenty minutes.

Notice whether he finds Back, the search box, and the Scene arrangement on
his own.

## What to ask afterwards

- What was the most interesting thing you found?
- Did anything connect to something you already knew?
- Was there anything you wanted to do and couldn't?

Ask these before any questions of your own. Then record his answers in his
words.

## Known rough edges he may hit

- There is no sound yet. Some edge panels say a playable demo is coming with
  the audio engine (M4).
- The year readout shows 2028 at the far right, which is the axis's margin
  rather than a real year (BACKLOG).
- In the densest years some names stay hidden until you zoom in. That is
  deliberate: they would otherwise print on top of each other.
- Only 39 artists are on the map. Anything he searches for that isn't here
  is data work for Track D, not a bug, but note it down: it is exactly the
  list of who to add next.
